### 2. Pakistan's first astronaut reaches China's space station
- **Facts:** China's Shenzhou-24 spacecraft launched at 08:18 Beijing time on
  25 November 2026 from Jiuquan, carrying Chinese astronauts Tang Hongbo and Wu Yi
  and Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40. It docked with the Tiangong space station
  about three and a half hours later. Ali, a Pakistan Air Force pilot and payload
  specialist, is the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut to
  board Tiangong. He is due to stay 5–10 days and return aboard Shenzhou-23.

### 3. Australia and Solomon Islands sign landmark treaty
- **Facts:** On 25 November 2026 in Canberra, Prime Ministers Anthony Albanese
  (Australia) and Matthew Wale (Solomon Islands) signed the Australia–Solomon
  Islands Treaty. It makes Australia the island nation's "primary security partner",
  states there will be no foreign military or security base on its soil, and commits
  Australia to A$740 million over a decade (A$670m in grants, A$70m in concessional
  loans) for education, health, policing, infrastructure and climate resilience.
  The deal follows reports that the Solomons will scrap its 2022 security pact
  with China.

### 6. Germany's Merz: Europe will "show Russia its limits"
- **Facts:** German Chancellor Friedrich Merz told the Bundestag on 25 November,
  during the final debate on the 2027 federal budget, that Russia was escalating
  "in the Baltic region, in Poland, on the Black Sea... in space" and that Europeans
  would "show Russia its limits". He defended a sharp rise in defence spending: the
  2027 budget foresees €139.4 billion in total defence spending, up €31.2 billion
  from €108.2 billion in 2026, rising toward 3.7% of national output by 2030.
